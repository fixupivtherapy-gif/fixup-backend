# 01 — Project Overview

## What it is

A bilingual (Spanish primary / English toggle) mobile-first web platform
that helps wholesalers and real estate investors close deals with
clarity and structure. It bundles:

- **Calculadoras** — deal analyzers (Fix & Flip, ARV, Mortgage, Section
  8, Subject-To, Seller Finance) plus a Claude-powered Rehab Estimator
  that analyzes property photos.
- **Recursos** — a bilingual real estate dictionary (target 350+ terms)
  and downloadable legal contracts in both languages (.docx).
- **Software** — operational tools: deal pipeline, buyers list, skip
  trace helper, AI-driven direct mail and cold-call scripts.

## Audience

Spanish-speaking real estate wholesalers and investors operating in the
US (with Puerto Rico jurisdiction baked into the contract templates).
Users move daily between Spanish (with sellers and family-network buyers)
and English (with title companies, attorneys, and institutional
buyers) — hence the bilingual contracts requirement.

## Voice & tone

Professional, grounded, bilingual. Not flashy. Not "guru-y." A serious
working tool. Visual language inspired by paper-and-ink documents —
warm bone background, restrained terracotta accent, hairline borders,
serif headlines.

See [[02 — Brand & Design System]] for the visual translation.

## Inspiration & differentiation

Inspired by the Hold My Hand Wholesale (HMHW) toolset but built as a
proprietary platform. Deliberately avoids the generic AI-default look:

**Won't do:**
- Centered gradient heroes
- Three-icon-card rows
- Safe blue/purple palettes
- Rounded-everything
- Emoji-heavy navigation

**Will do:**
- Asymmetric, intentional layouts
- Small-caps section dividers
- List-style navigation rows (not card grids)
- Subtle paper-grain texture
- One restrained accent color

## Brand identity

- **Name:** JD's Property Solutions
- **Wordmark:** typographic only (no symbol/mark in v1)
- **Wordmark treatment:** "JD" in display serif (Fraunces) + apostrophe
  in clay accent + "Property Solutions" in small-caps grotesk eyebrow

## Languages & locale

- **Default:** Spanish (`es`), targeting es-US / es-MX speakers
- **Secondary:** English (`en`), toggle in top-right
- **Currency:** USD throughout (US-based wholesaling)
- **Date format:** locale-respecting (DD/MM/YYYY for ES, MM/DD/YYYY for EN)
- **Contracts:** fully Spanish AND fully English template files — not
  translated UI strings around an English legal body. See [[05 — Contratos]].
